## The context

Taxd is a UK self-assessment and tax-filing platform competing in one of the most fiercely fought keyword markets in B2C SaaS - against HMRC itself, QuickBooks, FreeAgent and a long tail of accountancy firms all bidding on the same high-intent terms.

When they came to DomiSearch in August 2023, Google Ads was delivering - but unpredictably. Monthly spend was around £1.46K, bringing in roughly 1.9K clicks and converting at 1.34%. That translated to 26 monthly conversions: enough to prove the channel worked, but nowhere near the volume Taxd needed to compete at the top of the UK self-filing market.

## The challenge

Three problems had to be solved at once.

- **The conversion rate was too low to scale on.** Pushing more spend through a 1.34% conversion rate would have burned cash, not bought pipeline.
- **Budget wasn't keeping up with opportunity.** Taxd wanted to compete for 10–20× the share of voice during tax season.
- **Efficiency had to hold while spend grew.** CPA couldn't drift upward as the account scaled - or the growth would be worthless.

## What we did

### Account rebuild

Full restructure. Tight, buyer-intent-themed ad groups. Rebuilt negative keyword lists from eighteen months of search-term data. Eliminated the fragmented groups that were starving Google's algorithm of the signal density it needs to optimise.

### Quality Score lift programme

Tightened keyword-to-ad relevance across the top themes. Rewrote landing-page copy to the structure that converts and pushed Core Web Vitals into the green. Effective CPCs on the core category terms dropped before we'd spent a pound more.

### Bidding migration, done properly

Gated the move from manual CPC → Maximise Conversions → Target CPA over 90-day windows. Each lever only pulled once the data threshold hit - no premature automation, no learning-phase collapse.

### Landing page CRO, continuously

Monthly A/B tests on the highest-converting ad themes. The 1.34% → 4.52% conversion rate lift isn't just ads - it's the landing experiences behind them, iterated on for two years straight.

## The results

Two years on, the account looks like a different business.

- **Monthly conversions: 26 → 607.** A 23× increase, measured on a like-for-like basis against the August 2023 baseline.
- **Conversion rate: 1.34% → 4.52%.** 3.4× better - compounded from ad relevance, Quality Score and landing page work running in lockstep.
- **Click volume: 1.9K → 8.3K per month** as spend scaled with confidence.
- **Monthly spend: £1.46K → £22.2K.** Crucially, scaled *into* a machine that was already working - not pushed into a hopeful test.

That last point is the one most agencies won't tell you about. Scaling spend is easy. Scaling spend *while* scaling efficiency is the whole job. For Taxd, every extra pound of budget bought more conversions - not fewer.

## Where we are today

The engagement continues. Weekly account reviews, monthly strategy calls, quarterly business reviews. Taxd now competes - and wins - for self-assessment keywords it couldn't have afforded to bid on in 2023. And the machine keeps compounding.
