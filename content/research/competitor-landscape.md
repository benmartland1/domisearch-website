# Competitor Landscape — AEO / GEO / AI Search Services

Research snapshot as of **2026-04-21**. Use this as reference when writing homepage copy, service pages, pricing pages, and positioning decisions for DomiSearch.

The market splits cleanly into two camps:

1. **Done-for-you agencies** — sell AEO/GEO as a service (monthly retainer).
2. **SaaS platforms** — sell AI visibility monitoring and optimization tooling (monthly subscription).

Many players blur the line, but buyers shop them differently. DomiSearch needs to decide which camp it sits in (or how it straddles both) before page structure can be finalised.

---

## 1. Pricing landscape

### 1a. Agencies (monthly retainer)

| Tier | Price band | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Entry / SME | $200 – $1,500 /mo | Mainstreethost ($199–$999 billed annually), Embarque (from $1,499), Eskimoz, basic UK "AEO + GBP" packs (£800–£2,200) |
| Mid-market | $1,500 – $8,000 /mo | ClickReady ($1,500+), Click Intelligence UK (£1k / £3k / £5k tiers), Opace UK (£800–£2k), The SEO Works (£5k–£10k), Atomic AGI (£4k–£8k), Minuttia ($4k+) |
| Enterprise | $10,000 – $30,000+ /mo | iPullRank ($15k/mo, 6-month minimum), Push Group (£15k–£30k), Onely ($10k+), Intero Digital ($10k–$50k), First Page Sage |

**Takeaways:**

- Roughly 80% of agencies hide pricing behind "request a quote". The ones that publish it (Mainstreethost, ClickReady, Click Intelligence) use transparency as a differentiator.
- In the UK mid-market specifically, **only Click Intelligence publishes tiered pricing**. This is a wide-open gap.
- Entry-level annual-billed packages ($199–$399/mo) are how agencies pull in SMEs who can't afford discovery projects.

### 1b. SaaS tools (monthly subscription)

| Tool | Entry tier | Mid tier | Top tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| HubSpot AEO Grader | $50/mo | — | — |
| OtterlyAI | $29 Lite | $189 Standard | $489 Premium + Enterprise |
| Scrunch | $250 Core (brands) / $500 (agencies) | — | Enterprise custom |
| Peec AI | Starter (price-gated) | Pro / Advanced | Enterprise |
| Profound | Demo-gated | — | Enterprise (raised $20M) |
| Visby | AppSumo-listed, lifetime deal model | — | — |
| Sight AI | Not disclosed | — | — |

**Takeaways:**

- Two SaaS archetypes: **transparent self-serve** (Otterly, Scrunch — publish prices, offer trials) vs **enterprise demo-gated** (Profound, Peec — force a sales call).
- The AI monitoring SaaS market raised ~$77M between May–Aug 2025 (Scrunch $19M, Profound $20M). This is a crowded, well-funded tier; DomiSearch probably shouldn't compete head-on as a pure-play monitoring tool.
- Free audit / "analyze my brand" tools are **table stakes** as lead magnets — Profound, HubSpot, Otterly, Scrunch all offer them.

## 2. Universal page-structure patterns

### 2a. Agency service page (9 of 10 pages follow this skeleton)

1. **Hero** — one-liner positioning ("Get found in AI answers, not just blue links") + free consultation CTA.
2. **Trust bar** — media logos (Forbes, Entrepreneur, Fortune, HubSpot) OR client counts ("600+ brands", "97% client retention").
3. **"What is AEO / GEO"** explainer block — buyers still don't know what it means; this is almost always present.
4. **Stats hero** — "25% drop in search traffic by 2026", "400M ChatGPT users/week", "50% of SERPs now show AI Overviews". Used to manufacture urgency.
5. **Platform coverage grid** — logos for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Google AI Overviews, sometimes Grok / Meta AI / DeepSeek.
6. **AEO vs SEO comparison table** — nearly every agency has one. Often a table with "Traditional SEO" column and "AEO/GEO" column.
7. **Service breakdown** — entity optimisation, schema markup, llms.txt, citation building, digital PR, content restructuring, EEAT signals.
8. **Process / methodology** — audit → strategy → implementation → measurement (typically 4 or 5 steps).
9. **Case study with a metric** — "1,200 AI Overview appearances", "228% signup lift", "350% traffic growth".
10. **Pricing packages** (only ~20% of agencies show these) OR **"cost of waiting"** FUD section if not showing prices.
11. **FAQ**.
12. **Final CTA** — almost always "Book a free consultation / free AEO audit / free strategy call".

### 2b. SaaS tool homepage skeleton

1. **Hero** — benefit statement + product screenshot or animated demo.
2. **Platform / model coverage logos** — which LLMs they track (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Copilot, Meta AI, DeepSeek).
3. **Social-proof logo bar** — "Trusted by 2,000+ marketing teams" (Peec), "Used by the best marketers" (Profound).
4. **3–5 feature deep-dives** with screenshots: set up prompts → view data → pick winners → add brands → choose models → find sources.
5. **Testimonial / named customer** (e.g. Profound uses George Bonaci, VP of Growth at Ramp).
6. **Free tool / free trial CTA** — "Analyze my brand", "Start free trial", "Get AEO report".
7. **Pricing table** OR **"Get Demo"** gate.
8. **Footer** — API, docs, changelog, integrations, resources.

## 3. Messaging & positioning angles in use

Catalogue of the taglines and narrative frames competitors are running. Useful for avoiding clichés and finding white space.

- **"Be the answer, not just a rank"** — Opace, GreenBanana, Iconic Digital. The most over-used angle.
- **Cost-of-waiting FUD** — "25% search traffic drop by 2026", "first-mover advantage", "the window is closing". Used on almost every agency page.
- **Platform dominance signalling** — listing 6–9 AI platforms as a coverage checkbox. Has become commoditised.
- **AEO-as-evolution-of-SEO** — Iconic Digital, scandiweb ("one holistic strategy, double the impact"). Reassuring frame for SEO-buyer personas.
- **Vertical specialism** — Omnius (B2B SaaS / fintech), Focus Digital (accounting / medical / solo attorneys), Minuttia (fintech / healthcare compliance), Avenue Z (growth-stage startups). Strong differentiator in a crowded field.
- **Proprietary methodology branding** — Intero "GRO" (trademarked Generative Response Optimization), iPullRank "Relevance Engineering", Omniscient "Surround Sound SEO", Omnius "22-point GEO strategy", Focus Digital "Thought Leadership SEO". Naming your method is standard practice at the mid/enterprise level.
- **Scarcity positioning** — Omnius: "we onboard only 8 clients per year". Signals boutique, premium.
- **Transparency as differentiation** — Mainstreethost publishes $199/$299/$399/$999 tiers; Click Intelligence publishes £1k/£3k/£5k. Rare and effective.
- **Free tools as lead magnets** — HubSpot AEO Grader ($50 but free grader), Profound's "Analyze my brand", Otterly's free audits, Scrunch's site audit. Universal tactic — DomiSearch should plan for this at launch.

## 5. White-space opportunities for DomiSearch

Gaps observed in the research that DomiSearch could credibly own:

1. **Transparent mid-market UK pricing.** Click Intelligence is the only UK agency publishing a £1k/£3k/£5k ladder. Every other UK mid-market player forces a consultation. Publishing clean tiers is a procurement-friction killer.

2. **Productised one-off SKUs.** Almost no competitor sells a fixed-scope audit, llms.txt + schema sprint, or "AEO foundation in 30 days" as a buyable product. Everyone pushes retainer. A packaged entry product lowers the commitment barrier and builds a natural upsell into retainer.

3. **Free AEO audit tool as lead magnet.** Already table-stakes — Profound, HubSpot, Otterly, Scrunch all have one. DomiSearch needs this at or near launch.

4. **Verticalised AEO for specific UK sectors.** Omnius owns B2B SaaS / fintech. Focus Digital owns US accounting / medical / legal. The UK mid-market still has open lanes: legal, e-commerce, hospitality, local services, professional services, higher education.

5. **Outcome-based positioning.** Most pages talk about *activities* (schema, citations, llms.txt). Few talk about *outcomes* as the primary frame — e.g. "AI citation pipeline", "ChatGPT brand takeover", "20 AI-referred leads in 90 days or you don't pay". The category is crowded on features, wide open on results framing.

6. **Methodology naming.** If DomiSearch is going to play in mid-market or above, a named proprietary method (à la "Relevance Engineering", "GRO", "22-point GEO strategy") is expected shorthand — worth designing early.

## 6. Things to avoid copying

Patterns that are overused and now generic — DomiSearch should either skip or subvert these:

- The stats block with "25% traffic drop by 2026" — appears on every page, now feels manufactured.
- Listing 9 AI platform logos as a coverage flex — buyers have seen this everywhere.
- "Be the answer, not just a rank" wordplay — burned out.
- "AEO vs SEO" comparison table — useful for SEO explainer content, but not a differentiator any more.
- Generic "Book a free consultation" as the only CTA — pairs poorly with a buyer already forced into 3 other consultations this week.
