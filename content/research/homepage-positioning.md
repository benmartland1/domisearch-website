# Homepage Positioning — How to Give Google Ads and AEO Equal Weight Without Diluting Either

> **The question:** The current homepage H1 *"Be the brand AI recommends"* is strong for AEO but makes Google Ads invisible. How should the home page be framed so visitors know DomiSearch does both — without becoming a generic "full service" agency and losing the AEO edge that makes the brand interesting?

> **TL;DR recommendation:** Keep an AEO-forward hero — but reframe the *umbrella promise* at the top as "search across every engine, paid and AI" and make the service split explicit in a two-card fold below the hero (which already exists as `ServicesGrid`). Rename the eyebrow from "Google Partner · Shopify Partner · AI-first" to name both disciplines. Don't go full unified-generalist — mid-market buyers in 2026 are actively warned against "AEO-as-bolt-on" positioning.

---

## 1. What the 2026 competitive set is actually doing

I pulled homepage copy from 12 live agency sites on 2026-04-22. Four distinct positioning archetypes emerged:

### Archetype A — "AI-first, everything else subsumed"
- **Push Group** (UK, £15–30k/mo): H1 *"AI Marketing That Wins Awards Isn't Theory… It's Revenue!"* — Google Ads buried under "Performance Marketing". ([pushgroup.co.uk](https://www.pushgroup.co.uk))
- **Single Grain**: H1 *"AI-Native Marketing Company"* / subhead *"Your Buyers Search Differently Now. Your Marketing Should Change, Too."* — AEO & GEO listed first; Paid Media execution is card #4 of 6. ([singlegrain.com](https://www.singlegrain.com))
- **Risk:** Google Ads clients don't self-identify in the first 5 seconds. This is where DomiSearch sits *right now* and it's exactly your concern.

### Archetype B — "Outcome-first, channels invisible"
- **Impression Digital** (UK, Nottingham): H1 *"Performance Marketing Agency of the Year"* / *"Evidence into Action"* — channels only revealed on scroll. ([impressiondigital.com](http://www.impressiondigital.com/))
- **SEO Works** (UK): H1 *"Get More Customers Online"* / *"Award-winning marketing. Proven results."* ([seoworks.co.uk](https://www.seoworks.co.uk/))
- **Directive** (US B2B): H1 *"Rethink the Potential of Your B2B Agency"* — branded methodology **DiscoverabilityOS™**. ([directiveconsulting.com](https://directiveconsulting.com))
- **Power Digital**: H1 *"Power is a world-class, tech-enabled growth marketing agency"* / *"Transform uncertain into undeniable"* — method named **The Power Circuit™**. ([powerdigitalmarketing.com](https://powerdigitalmarketing.com))
- **Risk:** invisible to buyers searching for "AEO agency UK" or "Google Ads agency UK" — the very queries DomiSearch needs to rank on. Also zero AEO edge.

### Archetype C — "Named umbrella with explicit pillars" *(this is where the winners sit)*
- **Hallam** (UK, Nottingham): H1 *"Be chosen before the search begins"* / *"B2B marketing that makes you distinctive enough to be remembered, visible enough to be found before the search even starts and measurable enough to keep your finance team happy."* — nav uses a clean four-pillar frame: **Brand / Reach / Experience / Data**, with Paid Media, Organic Search, AI Search sitting as peer children of "Reach". ([hallam.agency](https://hallam.agency/))
- **Ignite Visibility**: H1 *"Your Trusted Agency for ROI-Driven Digital Marketing"* — three-pillar frame **Earned / Paid / Owned** media, all explicitly named on the homepage. ([ignitevisibility.com](https://ignitevisibility.com))
- **SEO Sherpa**: positions around *"Search Everywhere Optimization"* — tagline *"Where search happens, we get you found"*. Explicitly includes ChatGPT SEO (GEO), PPC, YouTube, TikTok under one umbrella. ([seosherpa.com](https://www.seosherpa.com))
- **Click Intelligence** (UK): H1 *"Award-Winning AI & Search Marketing Agency"* — just names both in the H1. Clean. ([clickintelligence.co.uk](https://www.clickintelligence.co.uk))
- **Strength:** buyers see both services named in the first fold. AEO edge preserved because it's upstream of the generic "search marketing" label.

### Archetype D — "Two separate pillars, no umbrella"
- **Impression** (again — also behaves this way in nav), **Direct Online Marketing** (*"Experience the DOM difference" / "No contracts. All results"*), **The SEO Works** — channels listed as a flat menu (SEO / PPC / Paid Social / Digital PR).
- **Risk:** buyer has to do the synthesis themselves. Increasingly dated in 2026 — "search everywhere" and "AI-native" language is swallowing this frame.

## 2. What the industry press & analysts are saying (April 2026)

- **Kevin Indig (Growth Memo, 2026):** *"2026 marks the beginning of the 'Agentic Era,' where AI stops just consuming the web and starts writing to it."* Predicts an *"extinction event in Q3 2026 for standalone AI visibility tracking."* ([searchwithsean.com](https://www.searchwithsean.com/post/digital-marketing-trends-2026-what-the-experts-are-saying))
- **Aleyda Solís, 2026:** brands must *"maximise brand and entity recognition across platforms — wherever their audience is."* — platform-agnostic, not channel-agnostic. ([searchwithsean.com](https://www.searchwithsean.com/post/digital-marketing-trends-2026-what-the-experts-are-saying))
- **eMarketer FAQ (2026):** *"Users under 44 average five search platforms, suggesting AI search is growing as a supplementary discovery channel rather than a replacement for traditional search."* ([emarketer.com](https://www.emarketer.com/content/faq-on-geo-aeo--where-ai-search-seo-overlap-2026)) — translation: buyers know they need *both* Google and AI.
- **Industry buyer guidance 2026:** *"Any agency treating AEO as a simple add-on to existing SEO services likely lacks specialized expertise; buyers should look for clear, distinct methodologies for entity optimization, topical authority, and AI-specific content structuring."* ([gen-optima.com](https://www.gen-optima.com/blog/top-aeo-agencies-2026/), [minuttia.com](https://minuttia.com/best-aeo-agencies/))
  - **Critical implication:** this is why a pure outcome-first ("we do growth") hero is now *dangerous* for DomiSearch — it signals bolt-on AEO, which is the 2026 red flag.
- **"Search Everywhere Optimization"** is rapidly becoming the default industry umbrella (Ahrefs, SEO Sherpa, Single Grain, Shoreline, Madx all use it). ([ahrefs.com](https://ahrefs.com/blog/geo-conferences/))

### What mid-market UK buyers want specifically

From the competitive scan + earlier VoC research ([aeo-audience-voc.md](./aeo-audience-voc.md)):

- **Segment A (panicked founders/SMB):** want to see AEO *and* Ads named — they're coming to DomiSearch specifically because of the AI-search angle, but need reassurance that the "I just want more leads from Google" box is also ticked.
- **Segment B (in-house SEO/head of growth):** allergic to "rebadged SEO". Want to see AEO treated as a *distinct discipline with its own methodology*, not an add-on. Archetype C plays to this. Archetype B (outcome-first) actively fails them.
- **Segment C (SaaS marketer chasing LLM citations):** primarily here for AEO, but is *also* usually running paid campaigns and would prefer one integrated partner. An umbrella frame wins them.
- **Segment D (agency/consultant):** doesn't really visit the homepage — they hit the AEO service page directly.

**The winning position is Archetype C.** Name the umbrella, name *both* pillars explicitly in the fold, and make the AEO page do the differentiation heavy-lifting downstream.

## 3. Three homepage options for DomiSearch (with tradeoffs)

### Option 1 — **Keep the current AEO-forward hero, reframe the umbrella**
Change H1 from *"Be the brand AI recommends"* → something like *"Win search across every engine — paid and AI."* Lead the subhead with the dual promise; make the service split explicit in the `ServicesGrid` (already does).

- ✅ Keeps the AEO edge that makes DomiSearch interesting (and differentiates from every "growth marketing" shop)
- ✅ Google Ads is visible by the second fold
- ✅ Still beats Archetype A (Push / Single Grain) because "paid and AI" is explicit up top
- ❌ Loses the punch of the current H1 (which is genuinely the strongest line on the site)

### Option 2 — **Dual-track hero (two peer H1s, split 50/50)**
Visually split the hero into *"Capture demand now."* (Ads) and *"Own demand forever."* (AEO), each with its own CTA. Like Hallam's "Reach" but one scroll earlier.

- ✅ Maximally honest — both services are visible equally
- ✅ Strong for founders who need both
- ❌ Risks looking generalist — every channel-split hero reads "full service agency" at a glance
- ❌ Loses the brandable single headline moment

### Option 3 — **Keep current H1, add a "demand capture + demand creation" sub-narrative** *(recommended)*
Keep *"Be the brand AI recommends."* as the H1 (it's the best line on the site), but:
1. **Rewrite the eyebrow** from *"Google Partner · Shopify Partner · AI-first"* → *"Google Ads + AEO · Google Partner · Shopify Partner"* — names both services in pill #1.
2. **Rewrite the subhead** to something like: *"DomiSearch runs Google Ads to capture the demand that exists today, and AEO to engineer the demand forming inside ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Two services, one search strategy."*
3. **Add a third line / CTA row** with two buttons: *"Google Ads Audit →"* and *"ChatGPT Visibility Check →"* — gives Ads a literal foot-in-the-door CTA next to AEO.
4. **Keep the stats bar exactly as is** — "£2M+ managed ad spend · 50+ brands · 6 AI engines · 8 wks to first AEO wins" already balances both beautifully.
5. **Name the umbrella on the page somewhere** — "Modern search" or "Search across every engine" as a section eyebrow lower down.

- ✅ Keeps the AEO edge and strongest single line
- ✅ Names both services above the fold
- ✅ Ads gets equal visual weight via the dual CTA row and eyebrow
- ✅ Sidesteps the "growth marketing agency" blandness of Option 2
- ✅ Avoids the 2026 "AEO-as-bolt-on" red flag by keeping AEO genuinely front-and-centre
- ⚠️ Requires a real rewrite of `Hero.tsx` — low cost, high signal

## 4. Service-page positioning (no dilution needed)

The service pages should *not* try to unify. Each stays specialist.

### Google Ads page ([`/services/google-ads`](../../app/services/google-ads/page.tsx))
- **Currently:** *"More revenue from Google Ads. Without wasting budget."* — this is good. Keep.
- **Add:** a short "Why combine with AEO" block *already exists* at line 171–184. Strengthen it: make the CTA *"See both services as one engine →"* linking to a short explainer (could be the homepage scroll or a `/how-it-works` page).
- **Do not** mention AEO in the hero. Let the page serve its one job: convince Google Ads buyers that DomiSearch is a serious Google Ads agency. AEO comes up once, on the way out.

### AEO page ([`/services/aeo`](../../app/services/aeo/page.tsx))
- **Currently:** *"Be the brand AI recommends."* — this is excellent, keep.
- **Already has** the "Do you also manage Google Ads?" FAQ answer at line 124–127 — which is the exactly right place to surface the cross-sell without diluting. Keep.
- **Strengthen** the Yamini Rangan "traffic is down" moment somewhere in the top third to pre-empt the *"why would I stop doing Google?"* objection (we're not telling them to stop — we're telling them to run both).

The service pages should mirror-ladder: each page's primary job is to win that segment's trust; each carries ONE well-placed sentence about the other service.

## 5. Naming the umbrella — two workable options

If DomiSearch wants a repeatable umbrella phrase (useful for the homepage and About):

- **"Modern search marketing"** — honest, 2026-accurate, SEO-friendly for category queries. Used sparingly across the category. Low risk.
- **"Search across every engine"** — tighter, more brandable, plays directly into DomiSearch's own name. Matches the industry shift to "search everywhere" without copying the Ahrefs / Single Grain wording.

**Recommendation: use both.** *"Search across every engine"* as the hero subhead frame; *"Modern search marketing"* as the semantic SEO phrase in metadata, H2s, and About.

What to **avoid**:
- *"Full-service digital agency"* — category-flattening, kills the AEO story
- *"AI-first"* — empty signal (flagged in [aeo-audience-voc.md §6](./aeo-audience-voc.md))
- *"Search Everywhere Optimization"* — already taken by SEO Sherpa / Single Grain, don't copy
- *"Growth marketing agency"* — triggers "AEO-is-a-bolt-on" red flag per the 2026 buyer guidance

## 6. Concrete copy block to drop into the hero (if Option 3 is chosen)

```
EYEBROW:  Google Ads + AEO · Google Partner · Shopify Partner

H1:       Be the brand
          AI recommends.

SUBHEAD:  DomiSearch runs Google Ads to capture the demand that exists
          today — and AEO to engineer the demand forming inside ChatGPT,
          Gemini and Perplexity. Two services, one search strategy.

CTAs:     [ Free Google Ads audit → ]   [ ChatGPT visibility check → ]

STATS:    £2M+ managed ad spend · 50+ brands served · 6 AI engines tracked · 8 wks to first AEO wins
```

This:
- Keeps the best H1 on the site.
- Names both services before the user scrolls.
- Gives Google Ads its own front-door CTA on equal footing.
- Plants a "two services, one strategy" thesis the rest of the page can ladder into.
- Costs ~15 minutes of code changes in [`components/Hero.tsx`](../../components/Hero.tsx).
