## The results in brief

Over 30 days of monitoring, the firm recorded **799 brand mentions** and **338 links to its website** in AI-generated answers.

That included **372 brand mentions in answers to questions that didn’t name the business**. Those show where the firm appeared when the question wasn’t already about it.

For one priority buyer question, the firm appeared in ChatGPT’s answer on **29 of 30 days**. Across the wider set of non-branded questions, appearances on Perplexity **increased fivefold** between the baseline and comparison weeks.

The client is not named, to protect its confidentiality. For the same reason, we do not publish the questions we track or the markets they cover.

## The starting point

Companies looking for a recruitment partner increasingly ask an AI assistant for a shortlist before they visit a single website. The firms named in that answer get considered. The firms left out often never hear about the search.

When we began, our first review found many relevant buyer questions where the firm did not appear at all. It was visible in some ChatGPT answers, but rarely on Perplexity and not at all in the Google AI Overviews we checked.

## What we delivered

### Mapping the questions buyers ask

We built a set of buyer questions covering the firm’s services and markets: who to use, how to approach a hire and how to compare recruitment options. We check those questions every day across the major AI assistants.

### Studying who gets recommended, and why

For each question we reviewed which firms were named and which websites the answers drew on. That showed where competitors were being recommended and which kinds of pages AI answers relied on.

### Improving the firm’s website and content

We then worked on the firm’s own website: technical improvements, clearer service information and new content that answers buyer questions directly.

That content is now doing its job. By 10 November 2026, **six new articles** on the firm’s website were being used as sources for AI answers.

## The headline result

For one priority buyer question, the firm appeared in **29 of 30 daily ChatGPT checks**, which is 97% rounded to the nearest whole percent.

It was not always listed first. Between 9 and 15 November 2026 it was listed first in 5 of the 7 daily checks.

## Five times as many Perplexity appearances

To measure progress, we compared the same 49 non-branded buyer questions across two weeks: **8–14 October** and **3–9 November 2026**.

| Engine | 8–14 Oct | 3–9 Nov |
|---|---:|---:|
| Perplexity | 2 | 10 |
| Google AI Overviews | 0 | 16 |
| ChatGPT | 28 | 18 |

Perplexity appearances rose fivefold, and Google AI Overviews went from no appearances to 16. ChatGPT appearances across the full set fell over the same two weeks. AI answers change from day to day, which is why we report every engine rather than only the best one.

### New questions, new visibility

In the baseline week, the firm was not named, linked to or used as a source for four of the tracked questions. By the later review, it was appearing or being linked to in answers to all four.

For one of them, the firm went from no appearances in the baseline week to being named and linked to in **all seven daily Perplexity checks** during the later review.

## What this means for a recruitment firm

A firm that relies on one AI assistant for its visibility is exposed when that assistant changes. This firm is now named across several engines, and hundreds of its mentions come from questions where the buyer had not yet named a provider.
