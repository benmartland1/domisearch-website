## Key Facts

- **Client:** an international recruitment firm. The client is not named, to protect its confidentiality.
- **Headline result:** appeared in 29 of 30 daily ChatGPT checks (97%) for one priority buyer question.
- **Wider progress:** 47% more appearances in non-branded AI answers, from 30 to 44, across 49 tracked buyer questions on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.
- **Links to the website:** 44% more links to the firm’s website from those answers, from 32 to 46.
- **New content used as sources:** six new articles on the firm’s website were being used as sources for AI answers by 10 November 2026.
- **Comparison weeks:** 8–14 October 2026 against 3–9 November 2026.

## The starting point

Companies looking for a recruitment partner increasingly ask an AI assistant for a shortlist before they visit a single website. The firms named in that answer get considered. The firms left out often never hear about the search.

When we began, our first review of the firm’s market found many relevant buyer questions where it did not appear at all. The firm was visible in some ChatGPT answers. On Perplexity it appeared only occasionally, and in Google AI Overviews we did not find it in any of the answers we checked.

None of those questions included the firm’s name. They are the questions a potential client asks before they have a provider in mind, so they show whether AI search introduces the firm to people who have not heard of it.

## What we did

### Mapping the questions buyers ask

We built a set of 49 non-branded buyer questions covering the firm’s services and markets: who to use, how to approach a hire and how to compare recruitment options. We check those questions every day on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

### Studying who gets recommended, and why

For each question we reviewed which firms were named and which websites the answers drew on. That showed where competitors were being recommended and which kinds of pages AI answers relied on.

### Improving the firm’s website and content

We then worked on the firm’s own website: technical improvements, clearer service information and new content that answers buyer questions directly. The aim was to make the firm easier for AI answers to understand, recommend and link to.

## The headline result

For one priority buyer question, the firm appeared in **29 of 30 daily ChatGPT checks**, which is 97% rounded to the nearest whole percent.

It was not always listed first. Between 9 and 15 November 2026 it was listed first in 5 of the 7 daily checks.

The exact question is not published, to protect the client’s confidentiality.

## Progress across all 49 questions

The headline result covers one question. To see the wider picture, we compared the same 49 non-branded questions on the same three engines across two weeks: **8–14 October 2026** and **3–9 November 2026**.

### Where the growth came from

| Engine | 8–14 Oct | 3–9 Nov |
|---|---:|---:|
| Google AI Overviews | 0 | 16 |
| Perplexity | 2 | 10 |
| ChatGPT | 28 | 18 |

The growth came from the two engines where the firm had been almost absent. Google AI Overviews went from no appearances to 16 in a week. Perplexity went from 2 to 10.

ChatGPT appearances across the full set fell over the same two weeks. AI answers change from day to day, and a single engine can move in either direction. That is why we track all three and report the full picture rather than the best-performing one.

## New questions, new sources

The gains were not confined to questions where the firm already had some presence.

**Four previously uncovered buyer questions.** In the baseline week, the firm was not named, linked to or used as a source for four of the tracked questions. By the later review, it was appearing or being linked to in answers to all four.

**One example in detail.** For one of those questions, the firm went from no appearances in the baseline week to being named and linked to in all seven daily Perplexity checks during the later review.

**Six new articles used as sources.** By 10 November 2026, six new articles published on the firm’s website were being used as sources for AI answers. That shows the new content was informing the answers, not just sitting on the site.

## What this means for a recruitment firm

A firm that relies on one AI assistant for its visibility is exposed when that assistant changes. Over these two weeks, the firm moved from being visible mainly on ChatGPT to being named on all three engines we track.

Each of those appearances came from a question that did not mention the firm. These are potential clients who were introduced to it while still deciding who to use.
