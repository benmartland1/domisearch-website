## What to Look For Before You Pick an Agency

Before the list, a quick framework. AEO is new enough that there's no standard accreditation, which means the quality gap between agencies is enormous. When you're evaluating anyone, ask these questions:

- **Do they track citations across LLMs?** Not just Google AI Overviews, but ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. If they only report on one platform, they're not doing AEO properly.
- **Can they show you a prompt audit?** A real AEO agency should be able to tell you exactly what AI engines say about your brand today, before you spend a penny.
- **Is their content strategy built for extraction?** AEO content isn't just well-written blog posts. It needs structured data, clear entity signals, and formatting that makes it easy for an LLM to pull a direct answer.
- **Do they separate AEO from SEO in their reporting?** If the monthly report looks identical to an old-school SEO report with a few AI mentions bolted on, that's a red flag. For the full measurement framework, see our guide on [how to measure AEO](/blog/how-to-measure-aeo-metrics-that-matter).

With that in mind, here are the five agencies worth your time.
